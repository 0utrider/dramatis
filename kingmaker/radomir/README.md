## Description

A soft-spoken frontier field medic turned death's quiet steward. Radomir still thinks of himself as a healer; his patients are just past the point where most people stop treating them. He checks pulses out of habit, closes the eyes of the dead, speaks politely to corpses, and his pockets jingle with coins. His ghostly thralls are "borrowed hands, returned with thanks." Obol rides along as familiar, conscience, and first passenger.

## Backstory

Radomir couldn't save a stray cat killed near a fresh crash site on Gaol, and then he heard it speak after it died. He has spent every day since learning to guide the restless dead home, leaving an obol on every body he lays to rest. A devout follower of Erastil, he believes community doesn't end at the grave: crash victims die light-years from their gods, and someone has to bring them home. He honors Pharasma as the rightful keeper of the dead. *"The Lady takes them. Old Deadeye asks that we bring them home first."* He keeps a ledger of every soul he has ferried, and one entry is still unchecked. A Pharasmin priest in Restov respects his intent but distrusts his methods. He is the kingdom's planned Magister.

- `companions/`: Obol, the familiar (keyart, portrait, `obol-token` as `.webm`/`.webp`/`.gif` plus static `.png`)
- `summons/`: six thralls (`borrowed-*-01` to `-06`), stag, rabbit, singer and the reeds, each with keyart and portrait. Tokens are animated smoke-ring loops (`*-token-01`, `.webm` for Foundry, `.webp` preview); static fallbacks are `*-token-02.webp` (Borrowed has none). For the Borrowed prototype token use the wildcard `summons/borrowed-token-*.webm` with Randomize Wildcard Images on.
- `fx/`: `life-tap.webp`, `inevitable-return.webp`, `thrall-strike.webp` (his magenta variant of the shared thrall strike icon), `release-thralls.webp` (macro icon: release all summons and thralls)
